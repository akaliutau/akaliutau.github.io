[GitHub repository](https://github.com/akaliutau/archivist)

Transforming books, articles, essays, and web sources into watchable short videos is typically expensive and prone to AI hallucinations. 

Current tools are not good: waste tons of resources on costly media generation before validating the script's factual accuracy. 

**Archivist** solves this problem via AI-driven research and engagement tool that enforces a strict, zero-hallucination methodology: research first, generate second, and render deterministically. This strategically separates creative reasoning from media rendering and limits expensive AI video generation to just intros and endings, producing highly grounded episodes for only $1–2 each.
